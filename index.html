What can I customize?+

Sarah Sweets specializes in custom presentation. Share your occasion, theme, colors, names or message when you inquire so the order can be planned around your vision.

What kinds of occasions do you create for?+

Weddings, baby celebrations, graduations, Eid, Ramadan, Hajj, teacher gifts, Mother's Day and many other events. Custom ideas are welcome too.

How do I request an order?+

Use the order builder on this site to organize your details, then contact Sarah directly through Instagram or by phone at 972-644-7253.

How early should I reach out?+

Because custom work takes planning, reaching out as early as you can is the safest option. Sarah can confirm availability for your specific date.
